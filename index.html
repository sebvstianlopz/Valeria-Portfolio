Questions

Good to know.

How soon can we start?

Usually within two weeks. After our first call you'll receive a strategy and the first content calendar before anything goes live.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn and Pinterest. Video is produced natively for each platform's format.

Do I need to be on camera?

Not at all. Many of my brands never appear on camera. We can build around product, place, team or UGC creators.

Is there a long contract?

Plans start with a 3-month period, the time it takes to see real growth, then continue month to month.

Can I review content before it's posted?

Always. You approve every calendar and video, with revision rounds included in each plan.

Do you only do one-off videos?

Yes, I also take on single brand films or launch campaigns. Send a message with what you have in mind.
